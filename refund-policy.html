DEWIFY / REFUNDS

Refund
Policy.

Last updated: 20 September 2026

1. We would rather help than argue

Buying a digital product should feel straightforward. If something goes wrong with a DEWIFY purchase, please reach out and tell us what happened. We will read the situation, explain the options clearly, and try to find a fair resolution.

2. When to contact us

For a purchase issue, please contact dewifystores@gmail.com within 14 days of purchase and include your Paddle transaction ID, the product name, and a short description of the problem.

3. What we can review

We may review requests involving a product that does not work as described, a compatibility issue that conflicts with the published product information, a duplicate charge, or another genuine purchase problem.

4. Digital products

Because DEWIFY products are digital and may be accessed immediately after purchase, refunds are reviewed based on the circumstances rather than treated like a physical return. This does not remove any consumer rights that apply to you under applicable law.

5. Our promise

We will keep the conversation human. You will not need to write a legal essay or fight through a maze just to explain a purchase problem. Give us the useful details and we will take it from there.

6. Contact

For help with a refund or purchase issue, email dewifystores@gmail.com. Please keep your receipt and transaction ID so we can locate the purchase quickly.
