Privacy
Policy.

Last updated: 20 September 2026

1. What we collect

The storefront may receive information required to complete a digital purchase, such as the customer's email address and transaction information provided during Paddle checkout.

2. Payment processing

Payments are processed through Paddle. DEWIFY does not collect or store full card numbers, card security codes or banking credentials on this static website.

3. Local storage

This storefront may use browser storage for basic preferences or cart state. This information stays on the customer's device unless a future feature explicitly sends it to a server.

4. Product delivery

After a successful purchase, the site may show a digital access link. The transaction and receipt information are handled by the payment platform and any delivery systems connected to the product.

5. Third-party services

Paddle and any services linked from a product or support flow operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Support

For privacy questions, email dewifystores@gmail.com.
